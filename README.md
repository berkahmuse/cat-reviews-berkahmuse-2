Entry for [Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990) — one funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, under 200 characters.

## The review

> One star. My human hogs the warm laptop keyboard all day, then shoves me off it like I'm the interruption. I live here. The keyboard is mine. He is the typo.

157 characters, one line. The complaint is specific and recognisable (the laptop keyboard), the voice is unmistakably feline (the human is measured by cat standards — the keyboard is the cat's, the human is the typo), and the sting lands in the last few words.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
